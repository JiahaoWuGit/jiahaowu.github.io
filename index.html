About

Jiahao Wu is a Postdoctoral Fellow at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University. His work centers on data-efficient AI for model training: selecting high-value prompts for reinforcement learning, condensing large recommendation datasets, and making learning systems more resource-conscious.

In parallel, he studies the safety of modern AI systems, including backdoor risks in graph condensation and full-stack safety across the data, training, and deployment layers of LLM agents. His work has appeared in leading conferences and journals, including NeurIPS, ICML, ICDE, The Web Conference, CIKM, and IEEE TKDE.

For research discussions and collaboration notyour_mason@outlook.com

Three connected threads drive my work: use data well, train efficiently, and understand the risks introduced along the way.
