01 · About

Research with efficiency—and responsibility—in mind.

Jiahao Wu is a Postdoctoral Fellow at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University. His work centers on data-efficient AI for model training: selecting high-value prompts for reinforcement learning, condensing large recommendation datasets, and making learning systems more resource-conscious.

In parallel, he studies the safety of modern AI systems, including backdoor risks in graph condensation and full-stack safety across the data, training, and deployment layers of LLM agents. His work has appeared at ICML, ICDE, The Web Conference, and CIKM, and in IEEE TKDE.

For research discussions and collaboration jiahao.wu@connect.polyu.hk
